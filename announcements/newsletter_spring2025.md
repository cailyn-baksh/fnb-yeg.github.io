# Food Not Bombs YEG Newsletter
## For Spring 2025

## Changing with the Seasons
As the weather warms up, our opportunities for expanding the organization are
growing. We are looking into reimagining and restarting some old projects,
including a Community Pantry to replace the Community Fridge program, and
looking for a new site to rekindle our Community Garden program. We are making
progress on both projects. We have finally gotten into a routine where we hold
our Community Meals every month as well.

## Programs
* Community Garden (in progress)
* Community Pantry (in progress)
* Community Meals
* Helping the Humans
* Fruit Collection (on hiatus, will resume in August)

## Service
FNB YEG has served many hundreds of households through our Helping the Humans
meal delivery program. We are also seeing an explosion in the number of
Birthday Club requests and we are consistently serving 40 people a month
through the Community Meals program at Mary Burlie Park.
